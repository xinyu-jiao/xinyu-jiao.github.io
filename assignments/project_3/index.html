About this catalog

This site is a color-driven catalog of my works. Instead of grouping projects by course or semester, each piece lives inside a color world: red, blue, green, and everything in between.

Color becomes a way to trace mood, medium, and questions across different projects — from haptic navigation devices for blind users, to interactive projections, to speculative interfaces.

Designed, coded, and collected by Xinyu Jiao.
