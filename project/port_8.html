Nature Spirits Blind Box Series

A blind box series that transforms nature-inspired spirits into collectible characters through garment design and soft forms.

Project Overview

Nature Spirits Blind Box Series is a collectible designer blind box series inspired by natural elements and traditional garment aesthetics.

Each character represents a gentle spirit of nature, translated into soft forms, calm expressions, and stylized wearable designs.

Concept & Inspiration

The series draws inspiration from landscapes, seasons, and organic materials found in nature.

Rather than referencing specific locations, the characters embody abstract natural moods such as calmness, growth, flow, and balance.

Character System

Each figure in the Nature Spirits series represents a different aspect of nature, such as water, plants, wind, or earth.

Their identities are expressed through garment silhouettes, color palettes, and subtle accessories.

Garments function as a visual language that connects each spirit to its natural element.

Garment & Color

The garment designs are inspired by traditional silhouettes and simplified for collectible figures.

Colors are derived from natural environments, including soft greens, river blues, earthy tones, and seasonal pastels.

Blind Box & Packaging

Each figure is packaged in a blind box format, allowing for random discovery and collection.

The packaging design reflects the calm and poetic tone of the Nature Spirits universe.
